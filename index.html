The Floor

Tempo Solar World sales training

Set up the floor

Enter one player per line, or fill it from your team roster. Each player gets their own square and a category to defend. If there are more players than categories, categories repeat.

How it plays

  1. Hit Randomizer to pick a challenger, or click any square to choose one yourself.
  2. The challenger picks a neighboring square. The duel is played in the defender's category.
  3. Each player has their own clock. Only the active player's clock runs. The challenger goes first.
  4. Host judges out loud: Correct switches the clock to the other player. Pass shows the answer while their clock keeps running.
  5. First clock to hit zero loses. The winner takes the loser's whole territory and keeps their own category.
  6. The winner can challenge again or send it back to the Randomizer. Last one standing owns the floor.

Projecting? Click Presenter window. Drag this game window to the projector, and keep the presenter window on your laptop. It shows each answer and runs the whole game, so answers never appear on the big screen early. Set your laptop to extend the display, not mirror it.

Keyboard in a duel: Enter correct, P pass, Space pause.
